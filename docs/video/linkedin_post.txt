For my PG Diploma in Data Science at the University of Calicut, I built Provenance: a supply chain risk console that explains every score it gives.

It takes shipment data, checks each batch with a quality gate, and scores every supplier each quarter on three factors: delay variability, late deliveries and destination country risk. Each flag shows which factor moved and by how much.

My first version raised 43 flags, many of them noise. Adding confirmation rules (a rise must be large, or hold for two quarters) brought that down to 20.

An AI agent investigates flags with read-only tools. Every number in its report must trace back to a tool result, or the report is rejected. It can only propose. Nothing happens until a named person approves it, and every decision goes into a hash-chained audit log.

Built with DuckDB, Dagster, dbt and React.
